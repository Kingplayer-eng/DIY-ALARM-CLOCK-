# DIY-ALARM-CLOCK-
A Hardware project!!
So basically its a esp32 powered alarm clock that will show time and ring alarm in morning or anytime . There is 3.5 inch tft screen with can be use by either touch or by joystick. It has a motion detector at top - Adafruit sensor it sense hand and shut of alarm . There is also a kill switch at back which can turn off everything . It will be powered by lipo rechargeable batteries and charging module to charge it of course. I will not add buzzer as it literally irritate your hearing instead i added a amplifier with speaker .
